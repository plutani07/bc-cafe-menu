# Food photos

Drop a photo here named after the menu item, lowercase with dashes, as a `.jpg`:

| Item in menu.txt | Photo file |
|---|---|
| Turkey Sandwich | `turkey-sandwich.jpg` |
| Mac and Cheese | `mac-and-cheese.jpg` |
| Chicken Caesar Salad | `chicken-caesar-salad.jpg` |

Square photos look best. Items without a photo show a food emoji instead.
